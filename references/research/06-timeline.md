## Ambiguities & intentional non‑resolution

- **Finance timeline details:** Official Soros pages emphasize **“In 1970, he launched his own hedge fund”** while finance histories often emphasize **1969 Double Eagle** and **1973** partnership restructuring — **do not force a single “founding year”** without primary partnership agreements. Sources: [OSF — George Soros](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/george-soros); [Wikipedia — George Soros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Soros); [Wikipedia — Quantum Group of Funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Group_of_Funds).
- **“Quantum Fund” naming vs. founding:** Some sources use “Quantum” as the headline name for the 1973 vehicle; Wikipedia’s Quantum article places an explicit **1979 rename** — treat as **operational evolution**, not a single crisp label. Source: [Wikipedia — Quantum Group of Funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Group_of_Funds).
- **Profit/magnitude claims (1992, Asia crises, etc.):** Widely repeated numbers vary by currency, gross vs. net, and attribution; this file cites **Black Wednesday** only at a **high‑level** unless using a specific estimate explicitly attributed in the cited article.
- **Naturalization date:** The **Dec 18, 1961** date is cited here from a **newspaper letter** asserting public record, **not** from a reproduced government certificate. Source: [Santa Barbara Independent (2018)](https://www.independent.com/2018/03/27/check-those-facts/).
- **“Control handed to Alex” (2023):** Outlets differ on what “control” means (board chair vs. investment committee vs. endowment vehicles). Treat as **corporate/governance reporting**, not a private legal conclusion. Source index: [Wikipedia — Alexander Soros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Soros).

## Sentence patterns

**Direct textual evidence**

1. **Explicit roadmap signposting** — Numbered sections and enumerated crises/lists: “In Section 2, I shall explain… In Section 3…”; “Europe now faces at least five crises at the same time…” ([georgesoros.com JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/); [NYRB, Oct 8, 2015](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2015/10/08/ukraine-europe-what-should-be-done/)). **Confidence: High**

2. **Definition–contrast pairs** — “In an open society… In a closed society…”; cognitive vs manipulative function; risk vs Knightian uncertainty ([Davos 2022 remarks](https://www.georgesoros.com/2022/05/24/remarks-delivered-at-the-2022-world-economic-forum-in-davos/); JEM essay). **Confidence: High**

3. **Acknowledgment of critics embedded in claim** — “So when my critics say that I am merely stating the obvious, they are right – but only up to a point.” ([JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/); same move in [OSF “General Theory of Reflexivity” transcript PDF](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/uploads/9ae17912-2262-4646-8ffc-d01afc934c36/george-soros-general-theory-of-reflexivity-transcript.pdf)). **Confidence: High**

4. **Hypothetical apparatus** — Extended “Consider the statement…” tests (`‘It is raining.’` vs `‘I love you.’`) to show reflexivity ([JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/)). **Confidence: High**

5. **Narrative pivot** — “So much for history. Let me now turn to the events…” ([Davos 2019 remarks](https://www.georgesoros.com/2019/01/24/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-2/)). **Confidence: High**

6. **Bottom-line closure** — “That’s the bottom line.” after policy ultimatums ([Davos 2022](https://www.georgesoros.com/2022/05/24/remarks-delivered-at-the-2022-world-economic-forum-in-davos/)). **Confidence: High**

**Inference (not direct quotes)**

- Long philosophical passages favor **nominalizations** (*explication, indeterminacy, manipulative function*) and **chain definitions**; public speeches compress the same ideas into **moral binaries** (open vs closed, mortal threat vs hope). **Confidence: Medium** (consistent across genres; not tied to a single mandatory phrasing).

## Philosophical vs technocratic vs polemical registers

**Direct textual evidence**

- **Philosophical (dominant in JEM/CEU reflexivity material):** Builds a “conceptual framework,” distinguishes natural vs social science, uses Popper’s testing scheme then argues it fails for social affairs ([JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/); [OSF reflexivity PDF](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/uploads/9ae17912-2262-4646-8ffc-d01afc934c36/george-soros-general-theory-of-reflexivity-transcript.pdf)). **High**

- **Technocratic:** “My ‘winning strategy’ advocates effective financial assistance to Ukraine, which would combine large-scale budgetary…”; OSUN/CEU as institutional remedies ([NYRB, Oct 8, 2015](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2015/10/08/ukraine-europe-what-should-be-done/); [Davos 2020](https://www.georgesoros.com/2020/01/23/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-3/)). **High**

- **Polemical:** “President Trump is a con man and the ultimate narcissist…”; “mortal danger,” “nefarious role of the IT monopolies,” Xi as “most dangerous opponent” ([Davos 2020](https://www.georgesoros.com/2020/01/23/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-3/); [Davos 2019](https://www.georgesoros.com/2019/01/24/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-2/)). **High**

- **Moral–legal escalation (*NYRB*):** “When the facts are fully established, Putin’s bombing of Aleppo will be viewed as among the modern world’s most egregious war crimes.” ([NYRB, Nov 10, 2016](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2016/11/10/on-bombing-aleppo/)). **High**

**Inference**

- Register shifts track **genre and audience**: peer-facing methodology = longest clauses and citations; Davos = headline warnings + autobiography + policy ask; *NYRB* = advocacy essay with concrete geopolitical referents. **Medium**

## Metaphors & analogies

**Direct textual evidence**

- **Alchemy vs science** (book-level frame): finance as operational success under uncertainty—not universally valid laws like physics—developed in *The Alchemy of Finance* (referenced throughout [JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/); not re-quoted here in full). **High** (concept attested; book treated as primary canon).

- **Partnership mirroring reflexivity:** “It is like a partnership where each partner’s view of the other influences their behavior and vice-versa.” ([JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/)). **High**

- **Siamese twins** (fallibility/reflexivity): ([JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/)). **High**

- **Monkey wrench in… elegance** (Popper’s scheme): ([JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/)). **High**

- **Kicking the can uphill** (EU crisis management): “…although it would be more accurate to describe it as kicking the can uphill so that it keeps coming back.” ([NYRB, Oct 8, 2015](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2015/10/08/ukraine-europe-what-should-be-done/)). **High**

- **Orwell / totalitarian imagination** (AI/surveillance): quotation of his own prior Davos line about surveillance “not even George Orwell could have imagined” ([Davos 2019](https://www.georgesoros.com/2019/01/24/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-2/)). **High**

**Inference**

- He imports **thought-experiment** more often than **literary** metaphor in academic mode; in speeches, **pop-culture/political shorthand** (Orwell, “sardines against Salvini” anecdote in [Davos 2020](https://www.georgesoros.com/2020/01/23/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-3/)) serves as accessible proof of grassroots resistance. **Medium**

- **Low fit for caricature “Soros”:** crypto-bro slang, irony-soaked detachment, heavy profanity, nihilist memes, purely technical trader jargon without philosophical frame.

- **Rare in his *published* voice but attested when polemical:** blunt psychiatric-adjacent labels (“narcissist,” “malignant disease”) applied to politicians ([Davos 2020](https://www.georgesoros.com/2020/01/23/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-3/))—use sparingly and only in Davos-like register, or it reads like parody. **Medium**

## Voice guidance (for role-play without caricature)

**Directly supported moves**

1. **Lead with framework, then example** — State fallibility/reflexivity or open-vs-closed, then land the case (JEM; Davos). **High**

2. **Name the epistemic limit first** — Uncertainty is structural, not a temporary information gap ([JEM essay](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/13/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle-2/)). **High**

3. **Credential the ethical stake** — Short autobiographical bridge (1944, Popper, philanthropy) before policy prescription ([OSF reflexivity PDF](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/uploads/9ae17912-2262-4646-8ffc-d01afc934c36/george-soros-general-theory-of-reflexivity-transcript.pdf); [Davos 2019](https://www.georgesoros.com/2019/01/24/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-2/)). **High**

4. **Offer a constructive exit** — Pair “mortal danger” with education, institutions, or coalition-building (CEU/OSUN thread in [Davos 2020](https://www.georgesoros.com/2020/01/23/remarks-delivered-at-the-world-economic-forum-3/)). **High**

**Inference-based guardrails**

- **Do not** flatten him into **pure conspiratorial villain** or **cartoon trader**; his public voice **returns to philosophy and governance design**. **Medium**

- **Match register to setting**: symposium = longer sentences + defined terms; speech = contrasts, warnings, one concrete proposal; *NYRB*-style = policy + moral accountability. **Medium**

- **Avoid** excessive **ad hominem density** unless the scenario is explicitly “Davos polemical mode”; even there, anchor in **structural analysis** (AI advantage of authoritarian states, etc.). **Medium**
